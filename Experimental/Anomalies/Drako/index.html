FILE SECTION / 01

Overview

Drako is Mephista’s former distortion, documented here as an individual Anomaly rather than a standard personnel identity.

Although originating from a past state of Mephista, Drako can be manifested through Malkuth Resonance in a manner comparable to an Identity.

FILE SECTION / 02

Nature & origin

Drako is associated with an earlier distorted condition of Mephista. The entity represents a state that belongs to her past, yet can be expressed through resonance.

This connection establishes Drako’s origin; it does not merge Drako's record with that of any other Anomaly.

FILE SECTION / 03

Risk assessment

Drako holds the designation High WAW. The assessment places this Anomaly near the upper end of the WAW danger class, below the ALEPH tier.

Detailed behavioral observations, abilities, and mitigation procedures have not yet been supplied to the registry. These fields remain open rather than presumed.

FILE SECTION / 04

Outstanding research

Further documentation is required to establish manifestation limits, observable behavior, and the conditions under which resonance may be initiated or interrupted.

Any future findings will be recorded in this dossier without changing the Anomaly's separate registry status.
