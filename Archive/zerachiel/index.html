Overview

Inarius Zerachiel was an Angel of the High Heavens, and a member of the Angiris Council’s extended ranks as the Archangel of Judgement. Among Angels he was admired for his intellect and his mastery over the Worldstone, but also feared for the same reason as well as his duty to judge souls without mercy or redemption.

History

However, the Eternal Conflict slowly hollowed him out. The war had no end, no victory and no purpose beyond repetition. To him, eternity began to feel like a cage. Where most angels endured the war as duty, Zerachiel grew resentful of it.

Zerachiel was in charge of training stars on the battlefield, though even this became a chore with eon after eon passing. With each new success and failure he hated the process more and more, attempting to push this responsibility onto lower angels or students by just handing them his studies, whilst pursuing a different path.

Personality

Zerachiel is known for his strict and disciplined nature, often pushing those around him to their limits in order to achieve greatness. He values strength, wisdom, and the pursuit of justice, and expects the same from those he trains. Despite his stern exterior, he has a deep sense of care for his students and allies, often going to great lengths to protect them and ensure their success.
