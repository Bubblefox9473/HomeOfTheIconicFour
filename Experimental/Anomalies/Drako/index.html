RESEARCH SECTIONS

01 / INITIAL OBSERVATION

Drako is Mephista’s former distortion, documented as a separate, manifestable Anomaly.

Drako’s origin is a past distorted state of Mephista. Dante can manifest the associated Identity using Malkuth Resonance, a Durante ability used to summon Sinner Identities when the corresponding Sinners are not nearby.

The archive recognizes Drako as a separate Anomaly, not simply a line item within Mephista’s personnel record. A common source does not imply identical nature, behavior, or danger.

02 / MANAGEMENT GUIDELINES

AN—001 is generally cooperative when compliance presents no personal loss. As an assist unit manifested through Dante’s Malkuth Resonance, he is notably talkative and usually willing to participate in operations.

  • COOPERATION — CONDITIONAL; STABLE WHILE SANITY IS MAINTAINED.
  • LOW SP — MAY ENTER AN INDISCRIMINATELY HOSTILE STATE, ATTACKING ALLIES AND ENEMIES ALIKE.
  • SANITY RECOVERY — OFFENSIVE ACTIONS USUALLY RESTORE SP; THE SUBJECT ENJOYS DESTRUCTIVE ACTIVITY.
  • RETURN TO CONTROL — UPON RECOVERING SUFFICIENT SP, AN—001 REGAINS FOCUS AND IMMEDIATELY RESUMES NORMAL COOPERATIVE BEHAVIOR.
  • MANIFESTATION — DANTE’S MALKUTH RESONANCE IS AN EXTERNAL DURANTE ABILITY, NOT AN—001’S OWN POWER.

CAUTION: Allowing AN—001 to regain Sanity through attacks may expose nearby allies to serious harm. His Hellfire may also remain hazardous during and after a loss of control.

03 / CAPABILITIES & OBSERVATIONS

HELLFIRE MANIFESTATION: AN—001 can generate supernatural Hellfire at will. These flames are exceptionally difficult to extinguish and do not die out naturally while imbued. AN—001 can continuously redirect and reshape his Hellfire, and can extinguish it voluntarily.

HELLFIRE IMBUEMENT / RANGE: The supernatural imbuement follows AN—001 within a large but unmeasured effective radius. Fire left beyond that range loses its Hellfire properties and remains as ordinary fire, which is easier to extinguish and can burn out naturally. The maximum radius has not been documented.

PYROKINESIS: AN—001 can manipulate existing ordinary fires, but cannot create ordinary fire from nothing. Only his own Hellfire can be manifested directly.

FIRE IMMUNITY: AN—001 is completely immune to fire from any source, including ordinary flames and Hellfire.

MOBILITY: AN—001 exhibits exceptional speed and flexibility.

E.G.O RESTRICTION: AN—001 cannot manifest E.G.O. His Hellfire and other innate abilities are not E.G.O manifestations.

Dante’s Malkuth Resonance is an external Durante ability for summoning absent Sinner Identities. It is not an innate capability of AN—001.

04 / CASE HISTORY

Drako originates from Mephista’s former distorted state. This historical origin is distinct from Dante’s later ability to summon an associated Identity.

HIGH WAW. This designation places Drako near the upper end of the WAW class; it is not an ALEPH classification.
