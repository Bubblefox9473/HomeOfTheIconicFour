ANOMALY INFORMATION

RESEARCH SECTIONS

01 / INITIAL OBSERVATION

An improbable outcome of Mephista, made possible through the consumption of Holy Ichor.

Aporia represents an outcome that ordinarily would have been improbable. The consumption of Holy Ichor is the known event responsible for making this divergent state possible.

The Holy Ichor event explains Aporia’s origin. Dante’s Malkuth Resonance is a separate Durante ability that can manifest the associated Sinner Identity when the Sinner is absent; it is not what caused that outcome to exist.

02 / MANAGEMENT GUIDELINES

AN—002 displays an authoritarian, openly condescending disposition, frequently talking down to both allies and enemies. Her speech and conduct suggest an expectation of superiority rather than an obligation to cooperate.

  • MANIFESTATION AUTHORITY — DANTE MAY MANIFEST AN—002 THROUGH MALKUTH RESONANCE, BUT CANNOT DIRECTLY CONTROL HER SKILL SELECTION OR ATTACKS.
  • COMBAT AUTONOMY — AN—002 SELECTS HER OWN SKILLS AND TARGETS; SHE MAY WILLINGLY ASSIST ALLIES DESPITE HER CONTEMPTUOUS MANNER.
  • SANITY RANGE — 0 TO 45 SP. NEGATIVE SP IS NOT POSSIBLE.
  • MINIMUM SANITY — AT 0 SP, AN—002 CONTINUES FIGHTING NORMALLY; NO NEGATIVE-SP STATE OCCURS.
  • DIRECT COMMAND COMPLIANCE — NOT APPLICABLE TO COMBAT ACTION SELECTION.

CAUTION: Manifestation is not command authority. Malkuth Resonance is Dante’s Durante ability, not an innate power or containment mechanism of AN—002.

03 / CAPABILITIES & OBSERVATIONS

PHYSICAL RESISTANCE: AN—002 takes equally low damage from Blunt, Slash, and Pierce attacks, making her exceptionally difficult to injure through conventional physical strikes.

ANATOMICAL RESTRUCTURING: AN—002 can reshape any of her limbs into dangerously sharp weaponry. She most frequently transforms her right forearm into an elongated blade.

SEVERE BLEED INFLICTION: The extreme sharpness and precision of her transformed weapons produce exceptionally deep wounds and devastating blood loss. The Bleed is attributable to the physical injuries rather than a separately established supernatural bleeding effect.

UNIDENTIFIED PIPE ARTIFACT: AN—002 carries a pipe in her left hand. Periodic activations restore her health and strengthen her. Its nature, mechanism, and exact activation conditions remain unknown. The pipe is confirmed not to be E.G.O.

MOBILITY: AN—002 moves at ordinary speed but exhibits considerable flexibility. Her fighting style emphasizes elegant, deliberate movements rather than rapid acceleration.

E.G.O RESTRICTION: AN—002 is unable to manifest E.G.O. Her anatomical transformations and pipe artifact must not be classified as E.G.O.

Dante’s Malkuth Resonance is an external Durante ability used to summon absent Sinner Identities. It is not one of AN—002’s innate capabilities.

04 / CASE HISTORY

Aporia is not documented as a remembered distortion. It is a divergent outcome resulting from the Holy Ichor event; additional circumstances remain unrecorded.

MID ALEPH. This designation puts Aporia within the extreme-risk ALEPH class, with a Mid subdivision assigned by NetherNova’s internal scale.
