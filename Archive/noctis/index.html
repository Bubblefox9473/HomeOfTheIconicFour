SECTION B / OBSERVED VALUES

Statistics.

Strength
Noctis possesses extraordinary physical strength, surpassing even that of his younger brother, Necro. His immense power allows him to overwhelm formidable opponents through sheer force, yet he exercises remarkable control over it. Rather than relying on brute strength alone, Noctis combines devastating power with precision and restraint, knowing when to hold back and when to unleash the full extent of his capabilities.
Combat Knowledge
Noctis possesses an exceptional understanding of combat, having mastered numerous martial arts, fighting styles, and weapon disciplines. His extensive knowledge allows him to adapt seamlessly between different forms of combat, recognize weaknesses in his opponents' techniques, and exploit them with remarkable precision. Whether armed or unarmed, he demonstrates extraordinary proficiency, combining technical mastery with devastating physical strength to maintain an advantage against even the most formidable adversaries.
Wisdom
Noctis possesses remarkable wisdom, distinguished not only by experience and sound judgment, but by his willingness to learn. Despite his considerable knowledge and authority, he remains receptive to unfamiliar perspectives, questioning his own understanding rather than stubbornly defending his beliefs. Every encounter offers an opportunity to grow, refine his knowledge, and broaden his perspective. His wisdom lies not in possessing every answer, but in recognizing that even the wisest individuals still have much to discover.

SECTION D / CAPABILITY REGISTER

Abilities & powers.

Limit Breaker

Limit Release

Noctis can voluntarily transcend his ordinary physical and combat limitations, entering a heightened state that dramatically amplifies his already extraordinary capabilities. Unlike abilities that require extreme circumstances or desperation to activate, Limit Breaker responds directly to his will, allowing him to unleash overwhelming power whenever he deems it necessary.

Limit Break Techniques

Beyond enhancing his capabilities, Limit Breaker grants Noctis access to exceptionally powerful finishing techniques. These attacks concentrate his extraordinary combat prowess into devastating offensive maneuvers, allowing him to deliver tremendous destructive force.

Foresight — Monado

Future Visions

Through the Monado, Noctis can perceive potential future events, allowing him to anticipate incoming attacks, recognize impending dangers, and foresee threats to himself or his companions. The blade belongs to his extended arsenal and can be summoned through the Gloves of the Azure Liberator. Although Noctis need not wield the Monado directly, it must remain physically on his person for these visions to occur.

Changing the Future

Rather than accepting his visions as inevitable, Noctis can actively intervene to alter their outcomes. By combining his foresight with his exceptional combat knowledge, he can adjust his strategies, counter otherwise unpredictable attacks, and prevent disastrous events before they unfold.

Gloves of the Azure Liberator

Arsenal Summoning

The Gloves of the Azure Liberator are gauntlets composed of stardust, granting Noctis access to an extensive collection of existing weapons. Through the gloves, he can summon an available weapon from his arsenal, causing it to materialize directly into his hand without needing to carry his entire collection.

Weapon Versatility

Noctis possesses remarkable adaptability in combat, drawing upon his extensive arsenal and mastery of numerous weapon disciplines to counter a wide variety of opponents. He has been seen creating opportunities to change weapons through carefully timed parries and defensive maneuvers, using the resulting distance to summon an armament better suited to the situation.
