Overview

Zerachiel is the Archangel of Judgement, known for his mastery over the World Stone and his role in training the Stars. He is a complex figure, often seen as both a mentor and a controversial character due to his methods and decisions. Despite this, he has earned respect from those who have trained under him, including Astra, who has become one of his most notable students.

History

Very little is known about Zerachiel’s early life. What is known is that he once trained several notable figures, including Astra, who would go on to become one of his most prominent students.

Over the years, Zerachiel has been involved in various conflicts and has played a significant role in shaping the course of events in the world. His actions have often been guided by his sense of justice and his desire to maintain balance, even if it means making difficult decisions.

Personality

Zerachiel is known for his strict and disciplined nature, often pushing those around him to their limits in order to achieve greatness. He values strength, wisdom, and the pursuit of justice, and expects the same from those he trains. Despite his stern exterior, he has a deep sense of care for his students and allies, often going to great lengths to protect them and ensure their success.
