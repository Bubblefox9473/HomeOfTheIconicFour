FILE SECTION / 01

Overview

Aporia is an improbable outcome tied to Mephista, made possible through the consumption of Holy Ichor.

The resulting state is manifestable through Malkuth Resonance, giving this divergent possibility its own place in the Anomaly Registry.

FILE SECTION / 02

Nature & origin

Unlike Drako, whose origin is rooted in the past, Aporia represents an improbable outcome. The consumption of Holy Ichor is the known event that allowed that outcome to become manifestable.

The dossier therefore treats Aporia as a separate Anomaly with its own risk assessment, not as a subsection of Drako's file.

FILE SECTION / 03

Risk assessment

Aporia holds the designation Mid ALEPH. Its assigned tier is above WAW, while the internal Mid grade distinguishes it from a High ALEPH assessment.

Detailed behavioral observations, abilities, and mitigation procedures have not yet been supplied to the registry. These fields remain open rather than presumed.

FILE SECTION / 04

Outstanding research

Further documentation is required to establish manifestation limits, observable behavior, and the conditions under which resonance may be initiated or interrupted.

Any future findings will be recorded in this dossier without changing the Anomaly's separate registry status.
