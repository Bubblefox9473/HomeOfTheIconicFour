01 / PRELIMINARY RECORD

DEFINITION / PROVISIONAL

The term Distortion describes a phenomenon in which a person's psychological crisis can trigger a radical transformation, often accompanied by powers reflecting their individual circumstances. Dante's field notes associate many observed cases with severe emotional collapse, while leaving open the question of what additional conditions cause a person to Distort.

The Limbus Company: Distortion (LCD) department investigates and responds to these incidents. Its On-site Investigative Reasoning team studies the underlying Inflection Point of each case: the circumstances and causes behind the manifestation. Research attributed to Moses describes early, middle, and late stages of progression. Some cases affect entire locations rather than only a single transformed person.

RESEARCH STATUS / These findings are drawn from field observations and Dante’s Notes. Progression, triggering conditions, and treatment remain subjects of ongoing investigation. LCD and NetherNova maintain the interview archive below as joint documentation.

LC:NN / INTERVIEWER#14 / RESPONDENT
LC:NN

For the record, could you confirm that you've personally experienced the Distortion Phenomenon?

#14

Certainly. Quite a.. vivid experience, although I wouldn't necessarily call it unpleasant.

LC:NN

Could you describe what you remember immediately before your transformation?

#14

There was the sound of an ominous bell. I remember that quite clearly, actually. I was at the sharpest edge of my lowest point. But it rang, and for a moment everything else seemed so terribly distant. Almost as though the world had decided to hold its breath.

LC:NN

A bell? That's consistent with several other accounts. Was there anything else?

#14

A warm voice. Such a lovely voice. Gentle, understanding... It felt as though someone had finally stopped, to try and understand just how lost I felt.

LC:NN

Were you able to identify the source of that voice?

#14

Hm? I couldn't give you a perfect description of a person. Though I hardly feel like that would matter. They simply offered me something. That is what mattered, I believe.

LC:NN

And what exactly was that?

#14

A way forward, I suppose. You see.. there is this darkness surrounding me at times... Something dreadful. I couldn't tell you precisely what would happen if it ever caught up to me, but in that moment, I knew I couldn't allow it.

LC:NN

You describe the darkness as though it were something pursuing you. Was this a physical sensation?

#14

Perhaps not in the way you're imagining. But it was there. And then, quite suddenly, there was a light. Not particularly blinding, nor overwhelmingly brilliant. Just... enough. Enough to show me that there was somewhere else I could go.

LC:NN

And you chose to follow it?

#14

Of course. Wouldn't you?

LC:NN

Our understanding suggests that Distortion manifests aspects of an individual's innermost self. Would you say your transformation reflected who you truly were?

#14

Ah, no, not entirely. Not at all, actually. Drako wasn't me. Not really. He was simply someone I could become.

LC:NN

Could you elaborate on that distinction?

#14

Well, it was easier, you know. Living alongside others, speaking with them, being someone they could understand. It was my surroundings that wouldn't allow me to be myself, a woman, so I became something, someone, tolerable. There were even people who knew Drako quite well without ever having met Mephista.

LC:NN

So you believe your transformation served as an escape from your original identity?

#14

An escape? Perhaps. Though I wouldn't put it quite so dramatically. It was simply the brightest path available to me at the time.

LC:NN

Do you regret taking that path?

#14

No. No, I don't believe I do. I remember those days rather fondly, actually. Fragmented, but fond.

LC:NN

You're aware that Distortion incidents have resulted in significant casualties and destruction?

#14

Naturally. Entire streets, buildings, countless lives... all of it can be lost because of a single person reaching their breaking point. I would never pretend otherwise.

LC:NN

Yet you continue to describe your own experience positively.

#14

Why shouldn't I? Something can be terribly dangerous without being terrible to everyone who experiences it. Sometimes, perhaps, it feels like salvation.

LC:NN

If you were to encounter that same voice again, would you listen to it?

#14

Hm... I suppose I would. It's never led me somewhere I believed I couldn't endure.

#14

...Excuse me. That little device over there. You've been recording this entire conversation?

LC:NN

Yes. All interviews conducted as part of this investigation are documented for subsequent review.

#14

Ah. I see.

LC:NN

Is that going to be a problem?

#14

No. Please, continue.

LC:NN

Very well. Returning to the voice you described earlier, do you recall anything specific about what it said?

#14

Not particularly.

LC:NN

Did it provide any indication of what would happen if you followed its guidance?

#14

Perhaps.

LC:NN

Could you explain what you mean by that?

#14

There's not much to explain.

LC:NN

I understand. Is there anything else about the experience you believe would be relevant to our investigation?

#14

No. Nothing else comes to mind.

LC:NN

Understood. That concludes our questioning. Thank you for your cooperation.

#14

Of course.
