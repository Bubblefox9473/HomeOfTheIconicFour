01 / PRELIMINARY RECORD

DEFINITION / PROVISIONAL

The term Distortion refers to a phenomenon in which an individual's inner state becomes manifest through a profound transformation. Its outward form and consequences vary from case to case.

Research into the phenomenon remains incomplete. This file is maintained by NNC–04 in cooperation with Limbus Company: Distortion (LCD), drawing on observations and recorded testimony rather than treating any single account as a definitive explanation.

NOTICE / The records below are intended to preserve individual perspectives. Testimony may be incomplete, subjective, or contradictory.
