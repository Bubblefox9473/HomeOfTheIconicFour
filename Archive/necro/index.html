SECTION B / OBSERVED VALUES

Statistics.

Endurance
Necro possesses remarkable physical endurance, capable of withstanding prolonged combat, severe injuries, and considerable physical strain. His resilience allows him to remain standing long after others would have collapsed. Though far from invulnerable, his ability to endure punishment is matched by a stubborn refusal to yield, especially when those he cares about are at risk.
Strength
Necro possesses extraordinary physical strength, capable of overpowering formidable opponents and wielding even the heaviest weapons with remarkable ease. His power is not merely brute force, however; years of handling and crafting weaponry have taught him to apply it with precision. Beneath his laid-back demeanor lies devastating strength, which he rarely feels the need to demonstrate unless sufficiently provoked.
Willpower
Necro possesses an indomitable will, capable of maintaining his resolve through overwhelming adversity, psychological torment, and seemingly hopeless circumstances. His determination stems not from blind optimism, but from an unwavering devotion to those he holds dear. Even when fear, exhaustion, or despair threaten to consume him, Necro refuses to surrender, often pushing himself beyond his limits through sheer force of will.

SECTION C / VISUAL RECORD

Appearance.

Necro is distinguished by his vibrant purple hair, a small crown floating above his head, and striking heterochromia, with one eye purple and the other a brilliant orange. His wardrobe reflects his fondness for purple, a color that dominates much of his attire. Contrasting this signature palette is a red scarf, an accessory of profound personal significance that he keeps close, carrying a meaning far greater than its appearance might suggest.
